Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026.

Amiibank does not collect, sell or share any personal data. The developer receives nothing from the app: no analytics, no crash reports, no accounts, no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your amiibo files, your key file, write counters, favorite games and the last N2 Elite scan are stored on your iPhone. The key file never leaves the device and is not included in backups.

Game Center backups

If you choose to back up, your collection and app settings are saved to your own Game Center account using Apple's saved games feature. Apple stores this data under your Apple Account; the developer cannot see it. Apple's handling is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Network requests

The app goes online only to download amiibo pictures and, when you tap Reload amiibo data, the latest amiibo catalog. Both come from the public AmiiboAPI project, served by GitHub. These requests contain no personal data, but like any web request they reveal your IP address to GitHub under GitHub's privacy statement.

NFC

NFC is used only while you are writing a tag or reading or writing an N2 Elite, and only for that device.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
