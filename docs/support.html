Common questions

Why can't I write to a tag? Writing blank NTAG215 tags needs your own key_retail.bin, imported in Settings. Amiibank never ships or downloads key files.

Can I rewrite a tag? No. An NTAG215 locks after it's written. Use an N2 Elite slot when you want to swap amiibo.

The phone doesn't find the tag. Hold the tag or N2 at the top back of the iPhone and keep it still until the sheet says Done. Thick cases can block NFC.

How do I back up my collection? Settings › Game Center › Back up. Restoring on another device adds what's missing and never deletes.
